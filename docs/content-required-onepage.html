Pharmatiya Health · Website

What we need from you

Vaelo Creative
7 September 2026

The website is built and finished. To fill it with your real material rather than placeholders, we need the items below. Nothing is published without your approval, and nothing has been invented — where something is missing, that part of the site says so or is left out.

If you do nothing else Answer the three starred questions and send us your logo and team photos. That alone finishes most of the site.

01 Answer five questions

A reply to this email is enough — about ten minutes

  • Is the product called NextGen AI or RWE - Builder? Your brief and your current site disagree. We need one public name.
  • Can we name your data partners publicly? Yes or no for Aetna/CVS, Cigna, Select Health, MedStar, Express Scripts, Komodo, Intermountain.
  • Who should receive enquiries? One inbox, or separate ones for new clients, existing clients and press.
  • How long does an internal compliance review take? Just the timing, so we can plan around it.
  • Where should the site be hosted? It can go anywhere — we will recommend an option if you would like.

02 Send us some files

However suits — email, a shared drive, or a link

  • Your logo as a vector file SVG, AI or EPS. The current one is a low-resolution image and looks blurry on modern screens.
  • Team photographs Headshots of the people who do the work. Phone photos in good light are fine. We will not use stock images.
  • Two or three anonymised case studies No client names needed. If writing them is the hard part, we will interview you and draft them for your approval.
  • Screenshots of NextGen AI With any real patient data blurred out.
  • Your study list A spreadsheet export. We have rebuilt the search from your current site — it works now, but has nothing to search.

03 Check what we have written

We drafted it from your documents — we need you to confirm it is accurate

  • Your three service descriptions Including the four stages we have written describing how each engagement runs.
  • What clients actually receive The deliverables listed under each service.
  • Your therapeutic areas We listed twelve from your published papers. Add any we have missed.

04 Tell us about NextGen AI

This is the only page we cannot write for you — every line is a claim about your product

  • What it does, in one sentence
  • How patient data is handled Your own wording, internally reviewed. This is what buyers will question first.
  • Which AI model it uses, and the version Your brief asked for open disclosure rather than a disclaimer.
  • What it does not do Being upfront about limits builds trust with this audience.

05 And a few small ones

Useful, but the site works without them

Job title and the year Pharmatiya was founded · confirmation that admin@pharmatiya.net is the right public address · a booking calendar link, if you want clients to book a call themselves · what the Quarterly Insights Digest covers and how often it goes out · a photograph or two of the office.

One security note, unrelated to the above: the access key for your study-list spreadsheet is visible in your current website's code — anyone viewing the page can read it and reach the sheet. Worth replacing whether or not you send us the list.
