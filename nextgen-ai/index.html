03/Where AI is used — and where it is not

The model does

  • Drafts synopsis structure
  • Summarises literature
  • Proposes cohort definitions

A researcher does

  • Approves the study question
  • Validates every method choice
  • Signs off all output before it leaves
  • Retains professional responsibility

The right-hand column is longer than the left, and that is the point.

04/Trust

01

No PHI uploaded

Protected health information is not uploaded to the system.

02

Human review mandatory

No output reaches a client without expert sign-off. Review is a stage in the workflow, not a check at the end.

03

Model disclosed

We name the underlying model and its version, and we say when it changes.
